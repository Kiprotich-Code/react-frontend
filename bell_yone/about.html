    Our Story

    Behind every great journey, there is a story—and ours stems from an intrinsic passion for discovery and a love for the unbridled beauty of nature. Founded on the premise to offer genuine experiences to the travelers, we bring you closer to the wonders of tented camps, safari getaways, and exotic vacations. Our journey had begun with one aim in mind—to make extraordinary travel experiences accessible to one and all.

    From an up-and-coming agency, over these past years, we have grown to become a trusted name within the travel industry. Imagine all your vacation dreams coming together in one place: think perfect, handpicked holiday packages, stays in luxury tented camps, and guided tours to reveal some of the world's best-kept secrets. We strive to present an experience of unbeatable value with the crème de la crème—be it across sprawling savannas of Africa or the chic coastal retreats along the Indian Ocean.

    We believe in travel magic—how it can change your life, build memories, and connect you with the world. That is why we focus on delivering experiences that are not just vacations but journeys of discovery. Whether you seek serenity, adrenaline rush, or cultural beliefs, we stand ready.

    Our team of enthusiastic travel lovers ensures, while planning your journey, every single detail is thought through and performed. We do everything possible to ensure the best offers on accommodation, expert tour guides, and a smooth booking process to offer you only the best travel experiences.

    Meet our CEO Robert, born and raised in a small village in Kenya, where the breathtaking landscapes shaped his worldview and fueled his aspirations. From humble beginnings, Robert's journey has been nothing short of inspiring. Today, his story continues to inspire, with every success of the company being a reflection of his tireless dedication and his commitment to sharing the wonders of his homeland with the world.

    Embark on a journey with us and discover the world like never before—with comfort, luxury, and adventure at every turn.

    Our story continues with you—let’s create your next adventure together!

    Hear From Our CEO

    Dear Traveler,

    Welcome to Bell Yone Tours and Travels! Every journey has a beginning, and mine began in a small, scenic village in Kenya, with the rolling hills, rich cultures, and breathtaking landscapes planting this seed of wonder in my heart. I was driven from a very tender age by my desire to share the beauty of my homeland with the world.

    At the same time, this has not been a journey devoid of challenges. Growing up, I faced financial hardship that could easily have derailed my goals. But through this drive to realize a different life for myself, determination fueled every step forward: first, school, then working my way up through the hospitality industry, and for over a decade, standing behind the scenes in hotels and lodges, learning the ropes of the industry.

    Hear From Our CEO

    These experiences taught me the real power of travel: seeing the pure joy light up on a visitor's face at the sight of a lion's roar in the wild or their first sunrise over the savanna. Actually, such sights are beyond mere sightseeing; they could be life-altering. And that is the kind of magic I wanted to create for others.

    When Bell Yone Tours and Travels was born, it wasn't about offering tours; it was about weaving a story into experiences that would leave an impact so long-lasting. I knew from day one that client satisfaction would be our compass. Every decision we make is dictated by the urge and zeal to ensure your experience with us is flawless, authentic, and unforgettable.

    As I think of our journey today, I feel proud-not just about where we started, but for the thousands of smiles, wonder moments, and memories we helped create. We may have grown as a company, but the values remain intact: passion, integrity, and commitment to you-our dear traveler. It is your satisfaction that motivates us, your trust that has kept us pushing the bar higher with each passing day.

    At Bell Yone, we're not here just to take you on a tour, but to make sure your adventure is everything you dreamed of, and more.

    Thank you for choosing to travel with us. Let's do more extraordinary journeys together!

    Warm regards,
    Robert Rono
    Founder & CEO, Bell Yone Tours and Travels
